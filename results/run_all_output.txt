===== Task04/q4_shared_media.py =====

===== Task05/q5_vlan_tag.py =====
802.1Q tag added to Ethernet frame: 4 bytes (TPID 2 + TCI 2).
Before segmentation: 1 shared Layer-2 broadcast domain (assumed flat VLAN).
After segmentation: 3 broadcast domains: VLAN 10 Office, VLAN 20 CCTV, VLAN 30 Guest.
Access ports are in one VLAN each; trunk carries tagged VLANs 10,20,30.
Guest ACL blocks VLAN10 and VLAN20 destinations and permits other routed destinations; apply upstream internet policy/NAT as appropriate.
